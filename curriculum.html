Curriculum

Eight weeks.
One craft.

A practical journey from product thinking and research to interface design, prototyping and portfolio work.

W1

Thinking in products

Briefs, constraints and how to make good decisions before you start designing.

W2

Research that matters

Interviews, jobs-to-be-done and finding insights that can actually influence a product.

W3

From insight to concept

Information architecture, user flows and wireframing.

W4

Visual foundations

Typography, colour, spacing and the principles behind strong visual systems.

W5

Design systems in Figma

Components, variants and tokens that make interfaces consistent and scalable.

W6

Prototyping & motion

Build interactive prototypes and learn how motion can make digital products feel clearer.

W7

Test, critique, iterate

Usability testing, feedback and refining your work based on evidence.

W8

Portfolio & the finish line

Turn your work into two strong case studies and prepare your final presentation.

Every week

Learn. Make. Share.

01

Live session

Learn the week's core concepts with a working designer.

02

Practical project

Put the ideas into practice through real design challenges.

03

Group critique

Present your work and learn through constructive feedback.
