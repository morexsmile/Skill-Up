02 — Curriculum

Eight weeks. One craft.

Every week combines a live session, a hands-on project, and a group critique. You build one product from week one to week eight — so the portfolio writes itself.

W1

Thinking in products

How great designers frame problems before touching a canvas. Briefs, constraints, and the anatomy of a good product decision.

W2

Research that matters

Interviews and jobs-to-be-done — turning messy conversations into insights you can actually design from.

W3

From insight to concept

Information architecture, user flows, and fast wireframing. Getting to the right idea before polishing anything.

W4

Visual foundations

Typography, color, and spacing with intent — why some screens feel premium and others feel off.

W5

Design systems in Figma

Components, variants, and tokens. Building interfaces that scale across a whole product, not just one screen.

W6

Prototyping & motion

Interactive prototypes and micro-interactions that communicate how a product should feel.

W7

Test, critique, iterate

Run usability tests, present your work for live critique, and turn raw feedback into sharper decisions.

W8

Portfolio & the finish line

Package your two case studies, tell the story of your process, and leave ready for real applications.
